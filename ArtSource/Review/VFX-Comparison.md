# Rank-one Vanguard VFX comparison

Watch `Emberfall-VFX-Before.mp4` first. It is **现有代码重建（非Unity实录）** from pinned Windows main `03422ab83d0ab6a8f84f2147a81d8aeb63a6cd5e`. `Emberfall-VFX-After.mp4` is **Blender优化预演**, a visual proposal not installed into combat.

Both videos use 1280×720, 24 fps, 9 seconds, the same static hero as scale reference, same neutral stage, camera, scale, lighting, and cast times (0.75/2.75 seconds Whirlwind; 5/7 seconds ground shock). Cast intervals are edited for comparison and do not claim legal skill cooldowns. Each effect runs at its actual sampled time scale; the optimized proposal has its own authored secondary-tail duration. No enemies or invented hit reactions are present.

Before retains the actual ring expansion/fade, two closed 3D crescents, four crystals, and moving root-tip sword ribbon. Production source executes to export geometry/TRS/time/colour/opacity/progress/style. Blender nodes translate FilledSpell UV grain, dissolve, edge tint and normal-light arithmetic; Blender colour management, normal interpolation, transparency sorting and camera-facing line tessellation remain approximations, not pixel-equivalent Unity shader output. The hero is deliberately static and does not reenact the baseline pose; the ribbon endpoints alone are sampled from the actual baseline pose. This is not character animation footage or Unity gameplay capture.

After explores rotating closed-section blades and a visible core, then ground contact, progressing crest/fault segments and chips. It is bounded geometry without an opaque full-screen smoke cloud. No hit timing, damage, range or balance change is claimed. Both videos use a cached native Blender stage plus separate native alpha passes with hero holdout occlusion; captions are assembled with Pillow/FFmpeg. Source scripts: `../BlenderPilot/render_vfx_comparison.py`, `assemble_vfx_video.py`, and `../VfxBaseline`.

The baseline frame export is independently reproducible: SHA256 `1dd484a473987317fc6dbb6e687cd00af8a19e41d8e5c4a78a88a763b2de9f23`. Unity 6 and device appearance, transparency cost, motion feel and visual acceptance are pending. The hero's provisional model quality is not evidence that the art pilot is accepted.
