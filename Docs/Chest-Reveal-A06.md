# A06 chest presentation

The choice screen presents three equal opportunities. Opening one uses the existing durable `OpenDungeonChest` transaction once. The other two fade out during the first 40% of the reveal; the final view replaces all three cards with one large reward and its full receipt. Desktop art is capped at 320 px (292–320 px at supported desktop heights); mobile uses its safe-area art column and crops weapon/back compositions. Gold, first collection and duplicate collection have distinct captions. Text scrolls independently of the fixed acknowledgement action.

Skip advances presentation time only. Restore displays the saved receipt immediately. An acknowledgement save failure keeps that receipt visible and retryable. The UI does not grant, reroll, equip, or discard rewards while drawing. Collection previews receive actual GUI-scaled pixel dimensions and use Back for wings, Weapon for weapon fashion, Full for general collection views.

Validation: `ChestRevealPresentationTests.Run(root)` exercises shared outcome copy/fade boundaries plus the real progression service's failed open, successful choice, restart, reroll rejection, failed acknowledgement and successful acknowledgement. Dependencies: the existing ProgressionTests support scaffold, GameTypes, CombatBalance, ProgressionService, HubTravelRules, MasteryCoreRuntime, TierRewardRules, TierRewardBand, ProgressionGoalState, and ChestRevealPresentation. MobileCollectionSourceTests and CollectionPreviewSourceTests check consumer wiring. Managed tests and compilation with older Unity reference assemblies do not establish Unity 6 rendering quality, touch usability, or device performance; those remain runtime acceptance items.
